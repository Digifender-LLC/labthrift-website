# You don't need a UPS yet

The computer can sit on a house outlet. A blink of the lights is a reboot. Skip a battery box this weekend.

A UPS is a battery box that keeps a computer on through a blink. First-timers hear they must protect the lab, and they buy one before the computer even has Pi-hole. The computer can sit on a house outlet. You don't need a battery box this weekend.

## A blink of the lights is a reboot

When the lights blink, the computer reboots. Pi-hole comes back. SSH comes back. That is [is the computer still on?](<leftover-box-still-on>). If you were in the middle of [keep the computer updated](<leftover-box-updates>), run the update again. That is annoying. It is not a reason to buy hardware.

## When a UPS is later

Come back to a battery box only if the computer already runs overnight backups, and a blink would leave a half-written copy. Even then, a USB disk you can re-run is cheaper than a UPS this month. That is [a backup of the house folder](<first-backup>). That day is not this weekend.

## Skip a lab UPS

Don't buy a lab UPS with extra outlets and a screen. Don't buy a rackmount UPS. Those are closet-later. A house outlet is enough for the computer.

## What next

If you are done for the night, [shut down the computer without yanking the plug](<leftover-box-shutdown>). If you have no second copy of the house folder, [a backup of the house folder](<first-backup>). Don't buy a NAS. Don't buy a rack.

## Skip

  * Skip a UPS this weekend. The computer can sit on a house outlet.
  * An old laptop battery is not a UPS. Confirm the charger: [see if the old laptop is actually plugged in](<leftover-box-plugged-in>).
  * Skip a lab UPS with extra outlets and a screen.
  * Skip a rackmount UPS. Those are closet-later.

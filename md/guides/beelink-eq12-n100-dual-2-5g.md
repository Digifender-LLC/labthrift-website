# Quiet dual-faster-port mini still sold new: Beelink EQ12

A quiet mini with two faster house ethernet ports for home services you leave on. Still sold new. Not EQ14, not a four-port firewall.

A quiet mini with two faster house ethernet ports, still sold new, for house services you leave on. 2.5G means faster house ethernet, not a 10G cage. This is the Beelink EQ12: N100, 16 GB DDR5-4800 SO-DIMM, 500 GB, **2× 2.5G** , HDMI ×2 + USB-C, Wi-Fi 6. TDP 6 W class / 25 W turbo. Amazon `B0CGHSSPSP`, `B0C1RQNRBZ`. Newegg `2SW-0012-00158` (first listed 6 Jun 2026) and `2SW-0012-000Y9`. Regional CZ shops still sell 8 GB and 16 GB. Skip 8 GB.

Distinct from [EQ14](<beelink-eq14-n150-dual-lan-proxmox>) (N150, often 1G, internal PSU, official max 16 GB DDR4). Dual-2.5G living-room class, not 4-NIC. If you need four ports, see [VP2430e](<protectli-vp2430e-n150-4x-2-5g-proxmox>) or [CWWK](<cwwk-n100-4x-i226-amazon>). The chip-class pick is still [N100 vs N150](<n100-mini-pc>).

## Skip

Skip 8 GB regional SKUs. Skip assuming EQ12 and EQ14 share a NIC. Skip chasing Minisforum UN100L or MS-01 as substitutes. UN100L was OOS on Newegg fetch; MS-01 is sold out. See [dual-LAN vs 4-NIC](<dual-lan-mini-vs-4x-2-5g-appliance>) if you are still choosing a class.

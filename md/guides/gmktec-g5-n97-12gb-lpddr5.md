# Skip the sold-out soldered 12 GB cube: GMKtec G5

Skip this sold-out 12 GB cube. One slow network port and soldered RAM, which means memory you cannot add later. Wait for a current N150 living-room box.

## Skip

Skip G5 because the title says N97 and the cart says N150-money. N97 is a 12 W Alder Lake-N, not Twin Lake N150. Skip soldered 12 GB, same class of trap as the in-stock [Mini S13 12 GB LPDDR5](<beelink-mini-s13-lpddr5-vs-ddr4>). Skip 2242 SATA as if it were a 2280 NVMe boot. Skip one gigabit port if the closet is already on 2.5G. Skip G5S N5095. Skip eBay lots. One-year GMKtec paper is not Beelink’s three-year.

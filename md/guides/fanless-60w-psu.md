# Fanless 60 W brick that is not a mystery barrel

Mini PCs and small fanless routers die on no-name 12 V bricks. Mean Well GST60A is a silent, Level VI, UL/TUV desktop adapter with a published datasheet.

Match voltage and the 5.5×2.1 mm P1J barrel before you click. The GST60A family also has 19 V and 24 V siblings (GST60A24-P1J and so on) if the host label is not 12 V. This is the brick for the [N100 / N150](<n100-mini-pc>), not a gamer PSU.

## Skip

Skip mystery barrel adapters with no datasheet. Skip RGB gamer PSUs. Confirm the host voltage, 19 V and 24 V GST60A siblings exist; don't force 12 V into a 19 V barrel.

# Cheap KVM-over-IP for a closet you don't want to sit in

Reach the old PC screen from the couch when it won't boot, without crawling into the closet. A $100 HDMI+USB box does that. Skip until you actually need it. A small N100 has no remote BIOS button.

Skip the $400 PiKVM V4 Plus unless you already want that stack. An [HDMI dummy](<hdmi-dummy-plug-headless>) is the EDID fix, not the KVM. The host is in the [N100 note](<n100-mini-pc>).

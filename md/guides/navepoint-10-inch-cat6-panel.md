# Skip the shielded 10-inch panel for the TRENDnet 12: NavePoint CAT6 STP

Shielded and Krone-only, about $18 more than the TRENDnet. The [TC-P12C6 punch-down](<trendnet-tc-p12c6>) stays the 10-inch default. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. Skip the cabinet-brand panel unless every drop is shielded and you will ground it.

SKU `00407266` is NavePoint’s own 12-port CAT6 STP panel for the 6U. 10 × 1.764 × 5.063 in. Maker store and Newegg (sold by NavePoint) both printed **$46.79** this pass. TRENDnet is the same width, unshielded Cat6, 110 or Krone, maker **$28.59**.

The cabinet-maker panel is the obvious add-to-cart next to the 6U. It is the wrong add. A closet of UTP Cat6 does not need STP. Shielding only pays if every drop is shielded and the panel is actually grounded. Krone-only is a second tax: the TRENDnet takes 110 or Krone, so the [Klein VDV427-300](<closet-punchdown-tool>) still works. Depth 5.063 in will hang in the NavePoint 7 in max mount. Width is the real 10-inch / 254 mm, not a 19-inch 10-inch-deep tray with a confusing title.

Amazon listing `B0CH1K346P` is live; no clean buy-box dollar on this fetch. Print the live tag. The CAT6A sibling `00407267` is the same 10-inch 12-port in STP Cat6A: more money, still not the closet default.

## Skip

Skip 00407266 because it matches the cabinet brand. Skip it for STP you will not ground. Skip the CAT6A 00407267 sibling unless the drops are Cat6A shielded. Skip 19-inch panels sold next to this one. You still need a Krone tool; it is not in the NavePoint box. Use the [Klein VDV427-300](<closet-punchdown-tool>). Used eBay lots are out. I haven't punched a 00407266.

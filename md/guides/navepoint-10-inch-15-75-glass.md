# Extra depth and a window, still not 19-inch: NavePoint 6U 15.75 glass

Extra depth and a window, still not 19-inch. A cheaper glass door is worse air, not a 19-inch switch. Buy glass only to look at LEDs. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. 19-inch is the normal rack width.

SKU `00406849` is the glass twin of the [15.75-inch 6U](<navepoint-10-inch-15-75>). Same 254 mm rails, same **10.62 in** max mount. Maker store **$183.99** this pass, in stock. Walmart matches that dollar, sold by NavePoint. Four dollars under the mesh sibling at $187.99. Default 10-inch start remains the [6U 11.8-inch perforated](<10-inch-mini-rack>) at $161.99. U is a rack height unit.

Outside: 12.3 × 13.8 × 15.8 in. Tempered glass, reversible, lock. Two shelves, 44 lb each. One 1U blank. 110.23 lb class. Pre-assembled. Hardware: 12× M6 screws, washers, cage nuts, two keys. Vented top, no fans. Newegg `2BA-001H-00228` is the same card; no clean street dollar on this fetch. No Amazon ASIN this pass. Print the live tag.

Glass already lost on the 11.8-inch 6U. Extra depth _and_ a window still does not make a 19-inch switch fit. If the wall takes 19-inch, the buy is the [StarTech 12U](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>).

## Skip

Skip buying G3 Pro as an N150. Skip any “G3 Plus / N150” URL whose live title says i3-10110U: that is this box. Skip 8 GB G3 Plus as a substitute. Skip treating dual-channel DDR4 as a reason to pay $300 for two cores. This is not a 4-NIC firewall. Listing roulette: [EQ14 1G vs 2.5G, G3 Plus vs G3 Pro](<n150-amazon-listing-traps>).

# Extra U and a window, still not 19-inch: NavePoint 9U glass

Extra U and a window, still not 19-inch. Glass is worse air for less money. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. 19-inch is the normal rack width. If the wall takes 19-inch, buy the [StarTech 12U](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>).

SKU `00406851` stacks the [6U glass](<navepoint-10-inch-glass>) trap on the [9U](<navepoint-10-inch-9u>) trap. Tempered glass door, still 10-inch-_wide_ rails, still 7 in max mount. Maker store **$195.99** this pass, about $8 less than the perforated 9U. A 19-inch switch still will not hang. U is a rack height unit.

Outside: 12.3 × 19 × 11.8 in. Reversible glass, lock, two shelves (44 lb each), one 1U blank, 110.23 lb class, pre-assembled. Hardware: 12× M6 screws, washers, cage nuts, two keys. Newegg `2BA-001H-00230`. Amazon `B0BNLZRZ2Q`. Print the live tag. About $34 more than the $161.99 [perforated 6U start](<10-inch-mini-rack>) this pass. The extra three U and the window don't change the rail width.

If you already measured 10-inch gear and three more U, the perforated [00406852](<navepoint-10-inch-9u>) is the 9U to buy. Glass 9U is the same job with a worse door. The 6U glass at $161.99 is the same dollar as the mesh start. Skip that one too unless you want to look at LEDs.

## Skip

Skip 9U glass because the photo looks like a show closet. Skip paying for height _and_ a window when the [perforated 9U](<navepoint-10-inch-9u>) is the air-first 9U. Skip any 19-inch glass wall box sold next to this one. If the wall takes 19-inch, stop shopping 10-inch cabinets.

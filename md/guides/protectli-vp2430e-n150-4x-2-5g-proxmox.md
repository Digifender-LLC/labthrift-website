# Fanless four-port closet firewall you can warrant: Protectli VP2430e

A fanless four-port closet firewall or router box with US support and coreboot. Not a living-room HDMI toy.

A fanless four-port closet firewall you can actually warrant. Not a living-room HDMI toy. 2.5G means faster house ethernet. SKU **VP2430e** : Intel N150 4C/4T, 6 MB cache, up to 3.6 GHz. One DDR5-4800 SO-DIMM. Protectli says they qualified **DDR5 to 64 GB** ; Intel’s datasheet still says 16 GB. M.2 2280 NVMe (PCIe 3.0 x2) plus optional 2.5" SATA. Fanless. Max power listed 45 W. Official bare configurator start was **$419** on 22 Aug 2026. Two-year factory warranty, extendable. No soldered RAM.

The older VP2430 shipped 32 GB eMMC. The “e” dropped eMMC in June 2026 to cut cost. Amazon.ca `B0F3DR4CQM` is VP2430 (not e). Don't send a US reader there. Prefer the US maker store. The existing [N100 / N150 roundup](<n100-mini-pc>) waves at “CWWK / Topton 4-NIC.” This is the named, warrantable N150 appliance in that class.

# 8-port vs 5-port 2.5G switch for a closet lab

Count the real endpoints: router LAN, NAS, desktop, AP backhaul, mini PC. Add two. That number picks the box.

Already know you want eight copper ports and no SFP+? Buy from the [8-port roundup](<2-5g-switch>). This page is just the port-count decision. All six boxes below are fanless and have no PoE. Netgear's 8-port lists 0 fans and 7.98 W max. TEG-S380 V2 max is 7.26 W.

## Skip

Skip 5-port leftovers with one 2.5G uplink and four 1G ports. Skip SODOLA / HASIVO toys. Skip buying five ports "to start" if the AP and the mini PC are already in the cart. Two unused 2.5G ports cost less than a second switch later.

## Skip

Skip this as a first closet buy if you don't have a VLAN. The [unmanaged 8-port](<2-5g-switch>) is cheaper and quieter to live with. Skip SG3210XHP-M2 as the "same ports" default. That SKU is 1U, 2 fans, 240 W PoE, about $420. Skip SODOLA managed toys.

# Quiet N150 with a 10G cage when MS-01 is gone: Protectli VP2440e

A small quiet box with a 10G fiber-style cage you can actually buy, when the Minisforum MS-01 is gone. Skip if you don't have a 10G job yet.

A small quiet N150 with a 10G cage when the Minisforum MS-01 is gone. SFP+ means a fiber-style cage. Skip if you don't have a 10G job yet. SKU **VP2440e** : N150, one DDR5-4800 SO-DIMM, Protectli-qualified max 64 GB. Two Intel X710-BM2 SFP+ plus two I226-V 2.5G RJ45. Fanless. Max 45 W. Official price **$499** on 22 Aug 2026. The original VP2440 with eMMC is volume-order only at $529.

10G optics and DAC are extra. N150 will not saturate two 10G links with IDS. This is still a 4-port appliance, not a living-room mini. Don't chase the MS-01 listing as a 2026 buy post. For plain 4× 2.5G without SFP+, the cheaper sibling is [VP2430e at $419](<protectli-vp2430e-n150-4x-2-5g-proxmox>).

## Skip

Skip buying 10G SFP+ because the SKU looks serious. If no flow fills 2.5G, stay on [VP2430e](<protectli-vp2430e-n150-4x-2-5g-proxmox>) or a [dual-LAN living-room box](<dual-lan-mini-vs-4x-2-5g-appliance>).

# Metal 8-port that costs more than the shipped TP-Link default: Netgear MS308 vs TL-SG108S-M2

A metal 8-port that costs more than the shipped TP-Link default. Buy Netgear only if the warranty story is worth the extra money.

A metal 8-port that costs more than the shipped TP-Link default. Both are fanless unmanaged 8× 2.5G, no PoE. MS308 lists 0 fans and 7.98 W max (Netgear spec table). Netgear.com **$109.99** (was $199.99). TP-Link MSRP **$79.99** , Prime-week street $49.99 (Tom's Hardware, 26 Jun 2026). Buy Netgear only if the warranty/support story matters more than about $30–60.

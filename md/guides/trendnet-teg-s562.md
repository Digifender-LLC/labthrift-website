# Quiet faster closet ports plus a real 10G cage: TRENDnet TEG-S562

Quiet faster closet ports plus a real 10G cage. Four faster copper ports and two 10G cages, no VLAN menus.

Quiet faster closet ports plus a real 10G cage. SFP+ means a fiber-style 10G cage for a short DAC or fiber to a file box. The [8-port closet roundup](<2-5g-switch>) sends people who need a 10G uplink to the TEG-3102WS (about $200). This 6-port box is the same uplink recipe with less copper and no management. Spec: 4× 2.5GBASE-T, 2× 10G SFP+, 60 Gbps fabric, fanless metal, desktop or wall, NDAA/TAA, lifetime paper in the US/Canada. No PoE.

Amazon street **$51.99** on the 22 Aug 2026 listing. Official-refurb sits at $45.99 (maker-backed 90-day). A footnote, not the hero SKU. Newegg shows one offer at $57.99 and another at $108.99; use Amazon or TRENDnet, not the inflated 3P. Maker page does not print a list price. Amazon does not print max draw; one Newegg offer text says 5.86 W.

## Skip

Skip SODOLA / HASIVO / no-name "8-port 2.5G + 10G SFP+" Amazon toys. Skip 5-port leftovers with one 2.5G uplink and four 1G ports. If you need eight 2.5G copper and no SFP+, stay on the [unmanaged 8-port default](<2-5g-switch>). If you already know you need VLANs, the TEG-3102WS in that table is the managed hop, not this box.

# Dual gigabit living-room mini, not the 2.5G listing: Beelink EQ13

A dual gigabit living-room mini. Don't confuse it with EQ14 2.5G listings. Buy EQ12 if you wanted two faster ports.

A dual gigabit living-room mini. Don't confuse this with EQ14 2.5G listings. 2.5G means faster house ethernet. The real EQ13 is N200 with two gigabit ports. N200 is a 4-core / 4-thread Alder Lake-N part, 3.7 GHz turbo, UHD 32 EU. Independent benches call it about 5% over N100, not the N150 bump. Dual LAN on this chassis is Realtek RTL8111H 1G, not I226-V. One DDR4 SO-DIMM, official max 16 GB. Dual M.2 PCIe 3.0: one x4, one x1. Internal PSU, IEC cord, no wall brick. That is the actual reason versus a Mini S12. Typical shipped config is 16 GB / 500 GB. Dual HDMI 4K, Wi-Fi 6, BT 5.2.

The maker EQ13 URL currently redirects to EQR5. Treat Amazon titles as hostile. Mini PC Guides still prints about **$240** for a 16 GB / 512 GB class config. Live US 16G / 500G cards on 23 Aug 2026: Amazon [`B0D5MGVFCL`](<https://www.amazon.com/Beelink-EQ13-PCIe3-0-Computer-Display/dp/B0D5MGVFCL>) and [`B0D5Y63XYD`](<https://www.amazon.com/Beelink-3-7Ghz-Desktop-Computer-Support/dp/B0D5Y63XYD>). Print the tag on the page. Ignore third-party Newegg cards at $999.

## Skip

Skip any “EQ13” click that describes N150: that is EQ14 bait. One Amazon search hit (`B0C339KVH9`) needs the title re-checked before anyone calls it EQ13. Skip EQ13 if you wanted 2.5G; that is [EQ12](<beelink-eq12-n100-dual-2-5g>). Skip it if you wanted N150; that is [EQ14](<beelink-eq14-n150-dual-lan-proxmox>) after you read the NIC. Skip 8 GB configs. Skip Newegg cards that title N100 on an EQ13 slug, or print $999 from a third-party seller. This is not a 4-NIC firewall: see [Protectli VP2430e](<protectli-vp2430e-n150-4x-2-5g-proxmox>). Listing roulette: [N150 Amazon traps](<n150-amazon-listing-traps>).

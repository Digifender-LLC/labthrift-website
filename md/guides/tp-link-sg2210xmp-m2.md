# Quiet switch that powers two house APs and a faster file-box link: TP-Link SG2210XMP-M2

A quiet switch that can power two house APs and still give a faster link to a file box, without a loud rack PoE heater.

A quiet switch that can power two house APs and still give a faster link to a file box. PoE means the access point takes power from the ethernet cable. 8× 2.5G PoE+ (30 W), **160 W** budget, 2× 10G SFP+, Omada smart. External 53.5 V / 3.37 A adapter. Max 180.1 W with 160 W PD. 226 × 131 × 35 mm. The point of this brick is no fans.

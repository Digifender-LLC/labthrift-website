# Terminate your own drop on a pass-thru plug: Klein VDV226-110

Terminate your own closet drop on a pass-thru RJ45 plug. This Klein is the ratcheting handle for that job, and it strips and cuts the lead too. It will not crimp a standard (non pass-thru) plug, and it does not replace the [110 punch-down](<closet-punchdown-tool>) that seats the [12-port panel](<closet-cat6-patch-panel>).

SKU `VDV226-110`. Maker page: ratcheting crimper / stripper / cutter for Klein Pass-Thru RJ45 plus standard RJ11 / RJ12. CAT3, CAT5e, CAT6 / 6A. Full-cycle ratchet. On-tool wiring diagram. 6.094 in / 15.5 cm, 14.4 oz / 408 g. UPC 092644690945. Replacement blade `VDV999-076`. Explicit skip: not for standard (non Pass-Thru) connectors.

## Skip

Skip using VDV226-110 on standard (non pass-thru) RJ45. The maker says so. Skip no-name Amazon pass-thru kits that don't name CAT6 vs CAT5e plugs. Skip buying only the 50-pack and hoping a $12 ratchet takes Klein Pass-Thru. Skip a used Fluke for twelve closet drops. Skip eBay lots.

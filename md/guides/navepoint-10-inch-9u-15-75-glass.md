# Taller, deeper, and a window, still not 19-inch: NavePoint 9U 15.75 glass

Taller, deeper, and a window, still not 19-inch. A cheaper glass door is worse air, not a 19-inch switch. Buy glass only to look at LEDs. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. 19-inch is the normal rack width.

SKU `00406853` is the glass twin of the [9U 15.75](<navepoint-10-inch-9u-15-75>). Same 254 mm rails, same **10.62 in** max mount. Maker store **$217.99** this pass, six dollars under the mesh sibling at $223.99. Default 10-inch start remains the [6U 11.8-inch perforated](<10-inch-mini-rack>) at $161.99. U is a rack height unit.

Outside: 12.28 × 15.75 × 19 in. Tempered glass, reversible, lock. Two shelves, 44 lb each. One 1U blank. 110.23 lb class. Pre-assembled. Hardware: 12× M6 screws, washers, cage nuts, two keys. Vented top, no fans. Amazon listing `B0BL5LLF8V` is live; no clean buy-box dollar on this fetch. Newegg `2BA-001H-00232` is the same card; no clean street dollar this pass. Print the live tag.

Glass already lost on the 11.8-inch 6U and the 11.8-inch 9U. Stacking extra U _and_ extra depth _and_ a window does not make a 19-inch switch fit. If the wall takes 19-inch, the buy is the [StarTech 12U](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>).

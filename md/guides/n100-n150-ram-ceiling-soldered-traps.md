# Buy RAM you can add later; skip soldered 12 GB cubes

Buy enough memory you can actually add later. Soldered RAM means memory you cannot add later. Skip 12 GB cubes. 16 GB is the floor for a mini PC.

Buy memory you can add later. Skip soldered 12 GB cubes. Soldered RAM means memory you cannot add later. Protectli VP2430e / VP2440e: official line is “Intel says 16 GB; we qualified 64 GB DDR5.” $419 / $499. EQ14 official max is **16 GB** DDR4 SO-DIMM. AOOSTAR N1 Pro is **12 GB LPDDR5 soldered** ($137–$175). ME Pro is **16 GB LPDDR5 soldered** ($559 official). G3 Plus 8 GB Amazon `B0DM58M5SD` is a trap. GMKtec G3S N95 8 GB / 256 GB showed $249.99 on Amazon similar-items 22 Aug 2026. N95, not N150, still an 8 GB trap. Minisforum UN100L is soldered 16 GB LPDDR5 and was out of stock on Newegg fetch; not a buy path.

Don't plan a VM stack on soldered 12 GB. Don't assume every N150 SO-DIMM board takes 32 GB because one Amazon table said so. S12 Pro `B0CBBV5NCT` claims max 32 GB; confirm in a teardown. I haven't opened one. ## Skip

Skip 8 GB as a default, soldered or not. Skip G3S N95 8/256 at $249.99 as an N150. Skip UN100L and MS-01 as in-stock heroes. Listing examples that lie about the chip: [Amazon traps](<n150-amazon-listing-traps>) (`B0F6CPHY48` is G3 Pro i3, not G3 Plus N150).

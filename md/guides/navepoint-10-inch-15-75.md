# Extra depth for a longer skinny switch, still not 19-inch: NavePoint 6U 15.75

Extra depth for a longer skinny switch, still not 19-inch. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. 19-inch is the normal rack width. Default remains the shorter 11.8-inch box. Buy this only if you measured a 10-inch-wide switch that needs more than 7 in of rail.

SKU `00406850` is the longer sibling of the [11.8-inch NavePoint 6U](<10-inch-mini-rack>). Same 10-inch-_wide_ rails. Maker store **$187.99** this pass. Newegg `2BA-001H-00233`, print the live tag. U is a rack height unit.

Outside: 12.3 × 13.8 × 15.8 in. Max mount depth **10.62 in** , not 15.75. Two shelves (44 lb each), one 1U blank, lock, perforated door, 110.23 lb class, pre-assembled. Hardware: 12× M6 screws, washers, cage nuts, two keys. Same kit idea as the 11.8-inch start. The extra four inches of box only help a 10-inch-wide chassis that would hit the back of the short cabinet.

Search “10-inch cabinet 15.75” and the trap is reading 15.75 as 19-inch-deep room for a 19-inch switch. The rails are still 254 mm. A Netgear MS305, a UniFi Lite 8, a 19-inch 12-port panel. None of those ears land. If the wall takes 19-inch, the buy is the [StarTech 12U](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>), not a longer 10-inch box.

## Skip

Skip 00406850 because the photo looks roomier. Skip any 19-inch 6U / 9U / 12U wall box sold next to this one. Those are a different rail. Skip the glass-door 15.75-inch sibling unless you want to look at LEDs; perforated is the same job. Skip eBay lots. The panel for this cabinet, once you are actually on 10-inch, is the [TC-P12C6](<trendnet-tc-p12c6>).

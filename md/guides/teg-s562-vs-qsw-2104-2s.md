# Pick today's cheaper quiet box with four faster ports and two 10G cages: TEG-S562 vs QSW-2104-2S

Two quiet unmanaged boxes with four faster ports and two 10G cages. Pick by today's price, not by brand loyalty.

Two quiet unmanaged boxes with four faster ports and two 10G cages. Pick by today's price. TRENDnet is the street-price win plus lifetime / NDAA paper. QNAP adds a loop-detect LED, a 16K MAC table, and a NAS-brand halo. QNAP store at $129 versus Amazon TRENDnet at about $52 is not a close fight unless B&H has the QNAP at $80 again. Both are fanless and have no PoE. QNAP max 12 W, 12 V / 1 A. Single-SKU notes: [TEG-S562](<trendnet-teg-s562>) and [QSW-2104-2S](<qnap-qsw-2104-2s>).

## Skip

Skip paying $129 for the QNAP when the TRENDnet is $52 and you don't care about the badge. Skip QSW-M2108-2C (1× PWM fan, QNAP-store $349 and OOS). Skip QSW-1105-5T (empty shelf). Skip no-name 4×2.5G + 2×SFP+ toys. If you need eight 2.5G copper plus SFP+, that is [a different hop](<2-5g-vs-10g-uplink-switch>).

# QSW-1105-5T vs TL-SG105S-M2: same five ports, usually more money

Same five faster ports as the cheap TP-Link. Skip the QNAP unless the price is close. The store still prints **$109.00** on a legacy unmanaged puck that is out of stock. Same five faster ports as the [TL-SG105S-M2](<tp-link-tl-sg105s-m2>) at $35–39.

Same five faster ports as the cheap TP-Link. Skip the QNAP unless the price is close. Same five faster ports as the cheaper TP-Link. QSW-1105-5T: five 2.5GbE RJ-45, unmanaged, fanless, 12 V wall brick. Maker max **12 W**. 16K MAC, 25 Gbps fabric, 12.5 Gbps non-blocking, 9K jumbo. Loop detect with a dedicated LED. 34 × 180 × 145 mm, a bigger desk puck than the TP-Link. Two-year paper. QNAP’s own spec page now labels it **Legacy**. Chip on Amazon cards is Broadcom BCM53161. Need 10G SFP+ for a NAS? That is [QSW-2104-2S](<qnap-qsw-2104-2s>), not this box.

QNAP US store SKU `QSW-1105-5T-US`, 23 Aug 2026: **$109.00** , out of stock. Newegg item [N82E16833831027](<https://www.newegg.com/qnap-qsw-1105-5t-us-5-x-rj45/p/N82E16833831027>) 301s to the same US SKU; don't treat a marketplace card as in-stock until you print it. Amazon cards [`B097CPP3J5`](<https://www.amazon.com/QNAP-QSW-1105-5T-Unmanaged-Broadcom-QSW-1105-5T-AMZ-US/dp/B097CPP3J5>) and [`B08CKT147N`](<https://www.amazon.com/QNAP-QSW-1105-5T-2-5Gbps-negotiation-unmanagement/dp/B08CKT147N>) exist; print the live tag if a 3P is selling. Don't wait a month or pay $109 for five 2.5G ports.

## Skip

Skip QSW-1105-5T because the NAS is QNAP. A switch does not have to match the NAS logo. Skip waiting on a QNAP restock. Skip Amazon 3P cards that only exist because the store is empty. Skip eBay lots. If you actually needed two 10G SFP+ cages, buy [QSW-2104-2S](<qnap-qsw-2104-2s>). If you counted past five endpoints, read [8 vs 5](<8-port-vs-5-port-2-5g-switch>).

# Quiet 2-bay on QNAP software with a faster port: QNAP TS-262A

Quiet 2-bay file box on QNAP software (QTS) with a faster-than-house ethernet port and room for fast solid-state cache. Skip it if you wanted cheaper TOS on an [F2-425](<terramaster-f2-425-review>) or DSM on a [DS224+](<synology-ds224-plus-review>).

Quiet 2-bay file box on QNAP software (QTS) with a faster-than-house ethernet port (2.5G) and two slots for fast solid-state cache. TS-262A-4G: Intel Celeron N5095 (4C/4T, 2.9 GHz burst), Intel UHD, AES-NI. 4 GB DDR4 SODIMM, two slots, 16 GB max. Two 3.5" / 2.5" SATA bays, hot-swap. Two M.2 2280 PCIe Gen3 x1 for cache or a flash pool. One 2.5GbE (2.5G/1G/100M/10M). Two USB 3.2 Gen2 Type-A (10 Gbps), two USB 2.0, USB copy button. 48 W brick. Sliding lid, no tools. QTS. Maker 3-year warranty. QNAP lab SMB on SSD RAID 1: 295 MB/s each way. That is their bench, not ours.

QNAP US store, 23 Aug 2026: diskless **$369** (was $439), in stock, SKU TS-262A-4G-US. Press (23 Jul 2026) said the A-series is a store exclusive; print the live cart. A Newegg card (N82E16822099175) printed $249.99 on fetch. Confirm the seller and a diskless title before you treat that as street. Skip eBay “TS-262” lots; that is the older N4505 box.

## Skip

Skip the old TS-262 (N4505, two cores). Skip HDD bundles. Skip [TS-264](<qnap-ts-264-vs-ts-262a>) unless you need dual 2.5G, HDMI, or the PCIe slot. That note is the $120 tax. Skip this box if F2-425 money is the budget. You are paying for QTS and two M.2 slots, not a faster CPU. Plan on more than 4 GB RAM. QNAP’s past firmware mess is a real reason some people stay on DSM; this note does not pretend otherwise.

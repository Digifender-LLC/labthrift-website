# Cheap UPS for a closet that stays on overnight

A small battery box so a blink of the lights is not a reboot. Skip a huge rack UPS. Skip this weekend ([you don't need a UPS yet](<leftover-box-no-ups>)). Some file boxes want a cleaner wave of power, and a USB cable lets the computer shut down politely if the battery runs out.

Size for runtime at 100–200 W, not the VA printed on the box. Plug a metal [1U strip](<closet-pdu-not-a-fire-hazard>) into the UPS. Hang both on a [12U 2-post](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>), not the carpet.

## Skip

Skip simulated-sine bricks under an active-PFC NAS. Skip a 1500 W rack UPS. Skip any unit with a sealed mystery battery and no HID USB.

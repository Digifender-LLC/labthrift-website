# More CPU in a compact 10G closet box, still not a rack server: Minisforum MS-A2

More CPU in a compact 10G closet box when you outgrow the [MS-01](<minisforum-ms-01-vs-used-1u>), without moving to a used 1U. The MS-A2 does that job with a fan and a higher price.

AMD Ryzen 9 9955HX, 16C/32T. STH: Intel X710 on the two SFP+ ports; one 2.5G Intel i226-V and one Realtek RTL8125: three NIC drivers from two vendors. USB-C on the rear is USB 3.2 Gen2 10 Gbps, not USB4, not Thunderbolt. That last bit matters if you wanted MS-01-style Thunderbolt clustering. Chassis is just over 1.7 L. Top and bottom vents: don't stack them. Small workstation fans. Not silent. Still not a 1U.

STH’s fetched review page did not publish a single idle watt. NAS Compares / storagediskprices commentary puts load well above the MS-01. Treat watts as **unmeasured by us**. I won't invent a closet idle.

## Skip

Skip a used 1U “because it also has 10G.” See [1U vs pizza box](<1u-vs-pizza-box-closet>) and [used vs new](<used-vs-new>). Skip stacking two MS-A2s on top of each other. STH called the heat bleed. Skip treating the rear USB-C as USB4.

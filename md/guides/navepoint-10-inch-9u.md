# Three extra U does not make a 19-inch switch hang: NavePoint 9U

Three extra U does not make a 19-inch switch hang. U is a rack height unit. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. 19-inch is the normal rack width. If the wall takes 19-inch, buy the [StarTech 12U](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>), not a taller 10-inch box.

SKU `00406852` is the taller sibling of the [11.8-inch 6U](<10-inch-mini-rack>). Same 10-inch-_wide_ rails. Same 7 in max mount. Maker store **$203.99** this pass. Newegg `2BA-001H-00231` printed $203.99.

Outside: 12.3 × 19 × 11.8 in. Perforated door, lock, two shelves (44 lb each), one 1U blank, 110.23 lb class, pre-assembled. Hardware: 12× M6 screws, washers, cage nuts, two keys. The 9U is three rack units taller than the start and about $42 more than the $161.99 6U this pass. The rails are still 254 mm.

NavePoint also lists `00406836`, a 9U 11.8-inch perforated cabinet without the two-shelf kit, maker **$187.99** this pass. Same trap: more height, same width. Gray 00406844 / Amazon `B0BL5F4V8P` was $195.99 on that card. Don't use gray as a reason to stay on 10-inch.

## Skip

Skip 9U because the 6U photo looks full. Skip any 19-inch 9U wall box sold next to this one. Skip the 15.75-inch-deep 9U unless you measured a 10-inch-wide switch that needs more than 7 in of rail. That is the same depth trap as the [6U 15.75](<navepoint-10-inch-15-75>). If the wall takes 19-inch, stop shopping 10-inch cabinets.

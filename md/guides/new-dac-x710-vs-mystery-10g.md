# Buy a new 10Gtek DAC and a named X710. Skip “works great” NICs.

Buy a new short DAC and a network card that names its chip. Skip random used 10G cards and mystery optics from a drawer.

The closet-cable sibling [SFP+ DAC for a closet hop](<sfp-plus-dac-closet>) already picks 10Gtek lengths. This note is the used-enterprise temptation: a gray “10G card, works great” listing with no chipset. You want Intel X710 / XXV710 or a known Mellanox SKU. You want a new DAC, not a mystery optic from a parts bin.

10Gtek SFP+ DAC 1 m, Cisco-compatible: Amazon ASIN B00WHS3NCA snap **$15.99**. Other 10Gtek 1 m listings ~$20.99 (CA). 3 m listings ~$28. Intel Ethernet Converged Network Adapter X710-DA2: Newegg snaps **$237.22** (14U-0045-00010) and **$279.25** (0XM-009Y-00017). ServerSupply new **$220**. 10Gtek-branded X710-BM2 dual SFP+ on Newegg from ~$165. Passive DAC: no fans. Vendor tables often put X710-DA2 in the few-watt / ~3–6 W band depending on optics. I didn't re-measure. Pairs with [CRS305](<mikrotik-crs305-vs-used-cisco-10g>), [QSW-2104-2S](<qnap-qsw-2104-2s-closet>), and [MS-01](<minisforum-ms-01-vs-used-1u>) SFP+.

## Skip

Skip “works great” 10G NIC listings with no chipset. Skip used random optics. No used eBay NIC price here. Skip a long passive if the other end is a NIC: the closet DAC note already warns under 3 m. If you have no cages yet, start with [2.5G copper](<2-5g-switch>) or [the Catalyst myth](<new-2-5g-vs-used-c3560-myth>).

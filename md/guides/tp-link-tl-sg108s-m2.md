# Eight quiet faster ports when file copies crawl: TP-Link TL-SG108S-M2

Eight quiet faster ports when file copies crawl. Buy the S-M2 lettering, not the older SG108-M2.

Eight quiet faster ports when file copies crawl. 2.5G means faster house ethernet, a bit more than twice old gigabit, and it still uses the cables you already have. Fanless metal, eight 2.5G ports, 40 Gbps fabric, 29.76 Mpps, 4K MAC, 12 KB jumbo, Cat5e 2.5G. Maker max draw 9.11 W. External 12 V / 1 A brick. 158 × 101 × 25.4 mm. Unmanaged: loop prevention, IGMP snooping, 802.1p. Plug it in. Wall-mount holes in the chassis.

Amazon listing MRP **$79.99**. pricehistory.app printed **$54.99** on 19 Aug 2026. SpecPicks still had $79.49 on 26 Jul 2026. PC Guide logged a June 2026 Prime print at $49.99 against a $129.99 strike. That is sale weather, not a $130 box. Count ports first: [8-port vs 5-port](<8-port-vs-5-port-2-5g-switch>). ## Skip

Skip the older **TL-SG108-M2** when the S-M2 is the $55 box. Skip 8-port toys with two 2.5G uplinks and six leftover 1G ports. Skip SODOLA / HASIVO no-names. Skip a managed Omada PoE chassis until you have APs that need that budget. Skip used 1G Catalyst for this job. If one flow already fills 2.5G, you want SFP+. That is the [10G uplink note](<2-5g-vs-10g-uplink-switch>), not this SKU.

# Hook-and-loop, not zip ties, in a closet

Zip ties are how you cut a DAC jacket six months later. A 100-pack of 8-inch hook-and-loop is the whole cable-management budget.

Color-code power vs copper vs [DAC](<sfp-plus-dac-closet>). Revisit after every add. Put a [TZe flag](<closet-label-maker>) on the lead before you bundle it. This lives on the [12U 2-post](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>), not behind the drywall.

## Skip

Skip zip ties on DACs and patch. Skip making the OOS VELCRO pack the hero.

# An extra tray for the 6U, not a 19-inch 10-inch-deep: NavePoint 10-inch shelf

The [NavePoint 6U](<10-inch-mini-rack>) already ships two 10-inch shelves. Buy a third only if those two are full. 10-inch here is the rail width of a skinny wall cabinet, not a 19-inch tray labeled 10-inch deep.

Buy SKU `00406855` only if the two kit shelves are full. Amazon `B0BNLZF1F1` printed **$31.99** this pass. Maker card has printed **$24.29** and gone empty. The 19-inch tray titled “10 inches (250mm deep)” will not hang here.

This tray is 10-inch _wide_. 44 lb class. Black powder coat. Box: 4× M6 screws, 4× M6 washers, 4× M6 cage nuts. UPC `810100701925`. Amazon dimensions on the card are a lazy 10×10×10 in cube. Trust the 10-inch-width series, not that cube. The 6U’s max mount depth is still **7 in** on the 11.8 in cabinet. A fat NAS brick will not sit even with a third shelf.

Search “NavePoint 10 inch shelf” and Amazon also returns 1U 19-inch cantilevers whose depth is 10 in. Example: universal tray `B0794QJR33`, ~$49, 17.5 × 9.75 in surface, “Fits all Standard 19" Racks.” That is the [19-inch 1U shelf](<closet-1u-shelf>) job, not this cabinet.

## Skip

Skip a third shelf because the photo looks empty. Skip any “10 inch” tray whose spec also says 19-inch EIA. Skip glass-door extras, RGB, and eBay lots. Skip hanging a 19-inch UPS on this tray. If the wall will take 19-inch, the whole cabinet was the wrong buy. See [12U vs 10-inch](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>).

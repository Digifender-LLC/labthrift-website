# Four quiet desk 10G links without a used Catalyst: MikroTik CRS305

Four quiet desk 10G links without bringing a loud used Catalyst into the closet. The CRS305 is a new, quiet desktop switch with four fiber-style cages.

Four SFP+ plus one 1G copper that also takes PoE-in. Dual boot SwOS / RouterOS. Two DC jacks plus PoE-in: three power inputs on a $149 list box. Included 24 V 0.8 A adapter. Input 12–57 V. Passive. Maker: max 10 W without attachments, 18 W with. STH on 120 V: idle **10 W** , max observed **13 W** , spec max 18 W. Case gets hot. No fans. STH: optics and DACs from Cisco, Juniper, Arista, Mellanox, and Intel coded without drama.

Maker suggested price **$149**. Amazon ASIN B07LFKGP1L snap **$136.02** (typical $144.90). STH review era: “almost always under $149.” Pair it with a named DAC in [new DAC + X710](<new-dac-x710-vs-mystery-10g>), not a mystery optic. If you need 2.5G copper plus two 10G cages, that is the [QSW-2104-2S](<qnap-qsw-2104-2s-closet>), not this box.

## Skip

Skip the used 10G Catalyst / Nexus for a closet. The ports are lovely. The fans and the 208 V aisle story are not. See [used vs new](<used-vs-new>) and [110 V brick vs 208 V 1U](<110v-brick-vs-208v-1u>). Skip treating this as a core L3 box; STH’s own tables show throughput falling off once you pile on filters and small packets. Layer 1/2 closet hop is the job.

# Learn RouterOS and VLANs when you want that work: MikroTik CRS310-8G+2S+IN

Learn RouterOS and VLANs when you want that work. The CRS310 has a fan. Skip it if you want a silent unmanaged switch.

Learn RouterOS and VLANs when you want that work. Skip it if you want a silent unmanaged switch. 8× 2.5G + 2× SFP+ (cages that also do 2.5G). Maker cooling type: **1 fan**. 21 W without attachments / 34 W max. No PoE-out. 24 V / 1.5 A adapter in the box. USB-A on the front. Rack ears included. This is not a fanless default.

MikroTik suggested **$219.00**. Newegg $219.00. Amazon US ASIN B0CH9NHFHS one snap **$209.70**. Amazon.ca 3P much higher. Ignore. Fanless managed path if you don't want RouterOS: [SG3210X-M2](<tp-link-sg3210x-m2>) or TEG-3102WS. Decision tree: [managed vs unmanaged](<managed-vs-unmanaged-2-5g-switch>).

## Skip

Skip this if you wanted fanless. That is the [unmanaged 8-port](<2-5g-switch>) or TEG-3102WS / SG3210X-M2. Skip CRS326-4C+20G+2Q+RM (~$999 when tested, 24-port heater class). Skip SODOLA / HASIVO toys that advertise "MikroTik features." Skip it as a first closet switch.

# Pick the cheap living-room palm box; confirm the LAN: Mini S12 Pro vs Mini S13

Pick a small living-room box. Confirm the network ports on the exact Amazon listing. Buy whichever is cheaper once the ports match.

Pick a small living-room palm box and confirm the network ports on the exact listing. Buy whichever is cheaper once the ports match. The [roundup](<n100-mini-pc>) already says buy whichever is cheaper. This note is the LAN and slot check. Mini S12 Pro: N100 4C/4T, 6 W / turbo 3.4 GHz, 16 GB DDR4 SO-DIMM. Amazon table on `B0CBBV5NCT` also says max 32 GB; confirm in a teardown; I haven't opened one. That ASIN is 1× 1000M LAN, Wi-Fi 6. Newegg `2SW-0012-00177` first listed 25 Jun 2026. A price-history snapshot 8 Jul 2026 showed Amazon **$429** (avg ~$188, low $149). Street is noisy; print the live tag.

Mini S13: N150, 16 GB DDR4, 1 TB, Amazon `B0D7GD7SLW`. Mini S13 Pro 16/500: Amazon `B0D5QXTFHH` showed **$358.90** in search; CA listing `B0DPMFPPTH`. Beelink’s 2026 Prime Day post still names Mini S12 / S12 Pro as current S-series. Living-room single-NIC class. Confirm 1G vs 2.5G per ASIN. Not a 4-port box.

## Skip

Skip treating S12 / S13 as a dual-LAN or 4-NIC appliance. For dual LAN see [EQ14](<beelink-eq14-n150-dual-lan-proxmox>) or [EQ12](<beelink-eq12-n100-dual-2-5g>). For a single 2.5G living-room node, [G3 Plus](<gmktec-g3-plus-n150-2-5g>) is the usual compare, once a live 16 GB ASIN exists. Skip 8 GB configs. Mini S13 RAM split: [skip the in-stock 12 GB LPDDR5 card](<beelink-mini-s13-lpddr5-vs-ddr4>).

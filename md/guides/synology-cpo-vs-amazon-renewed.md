# Buy a new or Synology-certified DS224+. Skip random “renewed.”

Official Synology Certified Refurbished exists. Amazon Renewed is third-party restore, not Synology CPO. The clean US buy on 22 Aug 2026 is a new diskless DS224+ at $299.99.

Official CPO: 2-year Synology warranty, 30-day returns, new panels and accessories (power adapter is refurbished), **not** eligible for Extended Warranty Plus. The live program page is the [EU storefront](<https://www.synology.com/en-eu/products/refurbished>). US SKU stock was not a populated cart on the US product page when fetched. A Newegg “Refurbished: DS224+” listed 24 Jul 2026 is sold by “Synology America Corp Marketplace”, treat as a maybe. Confirm seller and price; the page showed the $299.99 new band, not a clear CPO discount.

New DS224+ diskless: Intel Celeron J4125, 2 GB (max 6 GB), 2× 1GbE. B&H **$299.99**. synology.com US MSRP **$299.99** (Frontdesk Review, 27 Jun 2026). Access **14.69 W** , HDD hibernation **4.41 W** , noise **22 dB(A)** , 60 W 100–240 V brick, 2-year, Extended Warranty Plus in NA/EU. Those are maker numbers with Synology HDDs.

HPE Renew is a channel/VAR program. There was no live closet switch or ProLiant CPO page with a clean retail cart on 22 Aug 2026. There is not one to link. Amazon Renewed OptiPlex / EliteDesk is also not official HP or Dell CPO.

# Same money as mesh, worse air: NavePoint 6U glass

Same money as the mesh door, worse air. Buy glass only if you want to look at LEDs. Perforated stays the default. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. 19-inch is the normal rack width.

SKU `00406847` is the glass twin of the [11.8-inch perforated 6U](<10-inch-mini-rack>). Same 10-inch-_wide_ rails, same 7 in max mount, two shelves, one blank. Maker store and Amazon `B0BNLY4YJD` both print **$161.99** this pass, the same dollar as the perforated start. U is a rack height unit.

Outside: 12.3 × 13.8 × 11.8 in. Tempered glass, reversible, lock. 110.23 lb class, pre-assembled. The glass does not make the rails 19-inch. A 19-inch switch still will not hang. The [15.75-inch-deep 6U](<navepoint-10-inch-15-75>) is a different trap. This one is the show-window trap: same money, less mesh.

NavePoint also lists `00406831`, a glass 11.8-inch 6U at maker **$143.99** this pass. That card is the cabinet without the two-shelf kit. It is still a glass door on 254 mm rails. Don't use the cheaper glass box as a reason to leave the perforated start.

## Skip

Skip glass because the photo looks cleaner. Skip paying $161.99 twice in your cart for both doors. Skip any 19-inch glass wall box sold next to this one. If the wall takes 19-inch, the buy is the [StarTech 12U](<12u-vs-10-inch-closet-rack>).

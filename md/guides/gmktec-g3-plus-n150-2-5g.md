# Living-room mini PC with one faster port: GMKtec G3 Plus

A living-room mini PC with one faster house ethernet port. Not the router. Skip 8 GB. No live 16 GB listing on fetch.

A living-room mini PC with one faster house ethernet port. Not the router. Skip 8 GB. 2.5G means faster house ethernet. Official NucBox G3 Plus is Intel Twin Lake N150, 16 GB + 512 GB config. Maker store was **sold out** on 22 Aug 2026 at a $499.99 tag that does not match Amazon street reports. Treat $499.99 as list, not street. CPU: 4C/4T, 6 W class, burst commonly listed to 25 W. iGPU 24 EU, up to 1000 MHz on most G3 Plus copy. One-year warranty vs Beelink’s three-year. The [roundup](<n100-mini-pc>) already names this box; this is the SKU note.

Amazon `B0DM58M5SD` is N150, **8 GB / 256 GB** , dual HDMI, 2.5G. Don't recommend that config. Third-party 2026 reviews cite 16 GB / 512 GB around $199–$229 and ~10 W idle: not first-party, and the 16/512 ASIN `B0DM25BD7Y` returned **404** on fetch 22 Aug 2026. I won't invent an ASIN. Re-check a live 16 GB G3 Plus N150 listing before anyone clicks Buy. Don't use `B0F6CPHY48`: that slug says G3 Plus / N150; the live title is G3 Pro i3-10110U. See [listing traps](<n150-amazon-listing-traps>).

## Skip

Skip 8 GB. Skip `B0F6CPHY48` as N150. Skip this box as a 4-port or dual-LAN firewall, single-NIC living-room class.

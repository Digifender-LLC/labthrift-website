Skip the sold-out soldered 12 GB cube: GMKtec G5

Skip this sold-out 12 GB cube. One slow network port and soldered RAM, which means memory you cannot add later. Wait for a current N150 living-room box.

Skip the sold-out soldered 12 GB cube. Soldered RAM means memory you cannot add later. Maker page: Intel Alder Lake-N N97, 4C/4T, up to 3.6 GHz, 12 W class. UHD, 24 EU. 12 GB LPDDR5-4800 onboard, not a SO-DIMM, not 16 GB. 256 GB 2242 SATA, slot claimed to 2 TB. Dual HDMI 2.0 4K60. Three USB 3.2 Gen1. Type-C is 12 V / 3 A DC-in, not an extra data NIC.

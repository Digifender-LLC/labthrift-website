A metal rack power strip that is not a fire hazard

The closet failure mode is a $12 no-name strip under a UPS. Buy a UL-listed all-metal 15 A 1U strip with a switch cover, and plug it into the UPS.

Skip $400 ATS PDUs until you have two power sources. Hang it on the 12U 2-post. The UPS note is here.

Skip

Skip no-name 15 A strips without UL. Skip plastic $12 strips under a UPS. Skip Tripp Lite PDUMH15AT ATS (ASIN B000MNBWW0, $435.65) for a first closet: that is a dual-feed product, not a strip.

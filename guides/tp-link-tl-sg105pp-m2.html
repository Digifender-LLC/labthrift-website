Two Wi-Fi APs plus a faster NAS without a rack PoE heater: TP-Link TL-SG105PP-M2

Two Wi-Fi APs plus a faster NAS without a rack PoE heater. PoE means power over the cable.

Two Wi-Fi APs plus a faster NAS without a rack PoE heater. PoE means power over the cable. Five 2.5G ports. Ports 1–4 are 802.3bt Type 3 PoE++ at 60 W per port, 123 W budget. The fifth port is 2.5G data-only. Fanless metal. Idle 7.57 W; 143.37 W with 123 W PD (220 V figures on TP-Link US). External 53.5 V / 2.43 A brick. That brick will be the warm part.

Amazon US $140 (ASIN B0CLM8W5JY). Newegg.ca listed $199.99. Maker page has no USD cart price. Cheaper and quieter than the 1U Omada PoE racks. If you need eight PoE+ ports and 10G uplinks instead, that is the SG2210XMP-M2. First-apartment 1G PoE is still on cheap PoE.

Skip

Skip paying $82–100 for the MS305 when the SG105S-M2 is $35–39 and you have no support reason. Skip 5-port toys with one 2.5G uplink and four leftover 1G ports. Skip SODOLA / HASIVO no-names. Skip the MS308 if you counted under six. That is the 8-port tax on a closet that did not need eight.

Notes / Used or new / Buy paper you can call on a closet lot, not SmartNet you cannot transfer

Written 22 Aug 2026. Specs from the maker pages linked below. Prices from those stores. I haven't used these.

Buy paper you can call on a closet lot, not SmartNet you cannot transfer

On a closet lot you don't get Cisco SmartNet. You get a gray box and a firmware story. Buy new paper you can actually call.

Cisco SmartNet is a contract. It is not a transferable perk that rides along on a used Catalyst. Skip eBay “SmartNet included” listings. The replacements are a new TRENDnet or TP-Link with a published limited-lifetime policy in the US/Canada, or a new UniFi with UI Care sold on the same invoice.

TRENDnet TEG-S380: lifetime manufacturer protection, US/Canada only. Street $75–88. See the 2.5G myth post. TP-Link TL-SG108S-M2 Amazon copy says “Limited Lifetime Protection.” Street $55–80. UniFi Switch Ultra 60W: UI Care 5-year add-on $29/unit on the same store invoice. Switch $159 / $171 surcharge-incl. on the UI store 22 Aug 2026. Sibling note Ultra vs used Catalyst PoE.

TRENDnet TEG-3102WS (8× 2.5G + 2× 10G SFP+, web smart, fanless): official store $219.99. Amazon ASIN B0BPJZCQ5J. Existing 2.5G guide cited ~$200. Package contents list a 12 V / 1.5 A adapter. Some TRENDnet spec tables also print “max 152.3 W” and “internal power supply.” That 152.3 W line looks like a PoE-series leftover. Don't treat 152 W as closet draw. Believe the 12 V / 1.5 A brick in the box (18 W at the wall if you take the label literally). I haven't metered one.

Skip

Skip used Catalyst + “SmartNet included.” That contract does not transfer because a listing said so. Skip Amazon Renewed as a substitute for manufacturer paper. Skip inventing an HPE Renew closet switch. There was no live closet CPO page on 22 Aug 2026. The heater test is still used vs new.

A 110 punch-down tool so the 12-port panel is not a paperweight

A punch-down panel is steel until you seat the pairs. Klein VDV427-300 is the named 66/110 impact tool. Skip no-name lots that don't say 110.

Hang this next to the Cable Matters 12-port on the StarTech 12U. The panel copy already says 110 or Krone. A screwdriver is not a 110 blade. The console cable is for a switch that will not take an IP. This tool is for the pairs behind that switch.

Skip

Skip no-name Amazon “impact punchdown” lots that don't name 110 vs Krone vs 66. Skip a flat screwdriver on IDC. Skip making Newegg $79 the Klein buy when Amazon and Home Depot print $39.97. Skip Fluke D914-class tools for a 12-port closet. Skip Cable Matters 201065-6 and 202065-6 (those are console cables). Skip 180010-CAT6 (that is the panel). Skip StarTech CABSHELFV1U (that is the shelf). Used eBay lots are out.

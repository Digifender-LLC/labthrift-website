Skip the shielded 10-inch panel for the TRENDnet 12: NavePoint CAT6 STP

Shielded and Krone-only, about $18 more than the TRENDnet. The TC-P12C6 punch-down stays the 10-inch default. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. Skip the cabinet-brand panel unless every drop is shielded and you will ground it.

SKU 00407266 is NavePoint’s own 12-port CAT6 STP panel for the 6U. 10 × 1.764 × 5.063 in. Maker store and Newegg (sold by NavePoint) both printed $46.79 this pass. TRENDnet is the same width, unshielded Cat6, 110 or Krone, maker $28.59.

The cabinet-maker panel is the obvious add-to-cart next to the 6U. It is the wrong add. A closet of UTP Cat6 does not need STP. Shielding only pays if every drop is shielded and the panel is actually grounded. Krone-only is a second tax: the TRENDnet takes 110 or Krone, so the Klein VDV427-300 still works. Depth 5.063 in will hang in the NavePoint 7 in max mount. Width is the real 10-inch / 254 mm, not a 19-inch 10-inch-deep tray with a confusing title.

Amazon listing B0CH1K346P is live; no clean buy-box dollar on this fetch. Print the live tag. The CAT6A sibling 00407267 is the same 10-inch 12-port in STP Cat6A: more money, still not the closet default.

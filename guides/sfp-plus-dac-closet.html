SFP+ DAC cables for a closet 10G hop

The cheapest short cable between two boxes that already have a fiber-style cage. No glass fiber. No tiny optics to mix up. Skip if neither box has that cage yet.

Stay under 3 m for NIC runs unless the listing says otherwise. 10Gtek’s own copy warns against longer passives on NICs. Pair with hook-and-loop, not zip ties, a DAC jacket is easy to nick. If you don't have cages yet, read new 10G SFP+ vs 2.5G copper first.

Skip

Skip the 8 m passive (ASIN B0BGMYZJWY, listing $35.99) for NIC-to-switch closet runs. Skip random optics until a DAC will not reach. A fanless 2.5G copper switch is still the first buy if you have no SFP+ yet.

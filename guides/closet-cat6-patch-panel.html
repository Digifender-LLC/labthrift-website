A 19-inch 12-port Cat6 panel for the StarTech 12U

Turn twelve wall drops into twelve numbered ports, so moving one is a port change and not a guessing game. Hang a 1U 12-port Cat6 punch-down on the 19-inch 2-post. The 10-inch TRENDnet and StarTech panels don't fit that frame.

Wall runs should land on numbered ports, not on the switch face. Hang this on the StarTech 12U 2-post. Tie the slack with hook-and-loop. Keep existing in-wall Cat5e; that is the 2.5G cable note.

Skip

Skip TRENDnet TC-P12C6 on this 19-inch 2-post: maker width 254 mm / 10 in. That SKU is the 10-inch NavePoint note. Skip StarTech C6PANL4512, 10-inch wall-mount, 45-degree ports. Skip Cable Matters 180058 vertical mini (10 × 2.25 in, 89D bracket). Those fit a 10-inch cabinet, not the StarTech 12U. Skip StarTech C6PANEL24 as the default; the datasheet is a real 19.0 in / 483 mm 1U 24-port, but Newegg street was $87–$91.99 this pass. Skip empty keystone frames unless you are mixing connectors on purpose. You still need a 110 or Krone punch-down tool; it is not in the Cable Matters box.

Notes / Used or new / 1U is for a rack. A closet gets a pizza box.

Written 22 Aug 2026. Specs from the maker pages linked below. Prices from those stores. I haven't used these.

1U is for a rack. A closet gets a pizza box.

Used 1U is deep, loud, and wants rails, blanking, and 208-friendly PSU airflow. The closet objects are 1–2 L pizza boxes. Most used 1U servers remain a bad buy. These are the few shapes that are not.

STH: MS-A2 ~1.7 L, looks like a TinyMiniMicro. Protectli VP2420e: 5.75 × 5 × 2 in, optional 1RU shelf, fanless, max 24 W, $359. ASUS NUC 14 Pro (NUC14RVH): classic 4×4, toolless. ASUS eShop Core 3 100U kit from $429. Configured Ultra 7 155H Amazon B0D6RPYL9B snap $1,189. Liliputing: Windows idle 8.8 W. YouTube homelab review ~10.5–12 W idle. Beelink Mini S13 N150: Amazon B09DPV3D4N snap $339 (12 GB / 500 GB, 2.5G). Official Beelink EQ14 is $375 / $429 on bee-link.com with dual 1G, don't copy Amazon “dual 2.5G” titles against the official spec. The EQ14 NIC trap is already written in EQ14 dual LAN.

Pizza-box idle band from published reviews: NUC 8.8–12 W; MS-01 13–35 W (jdhodges); Protectli ~8–12 W (KB / HomeNetworkGuy). 1U: ~70 W+ idle typical. NUC and Beelink have small fans. Protectli is silent.

Skip

Skip used Dell R630 / R640 / HPE DL360 1U as a closet host. Skip the official EQ14 if what you wanted was dual 2.5G, bee-link.com lists dual 1000 Mbps. PSU rule: if it wants 208 V, it does not belong here. The original test: used vs new.

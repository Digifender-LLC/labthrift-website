Connect a 10G NAS and four faster PCs without a used Cisco chassis: QNAP QSW-2104-2S

Connect a 10G NAS and four faster PCs without putting a used Cisco chassis in the closet. The QSW-2104-2S is a fanless unmanaged box for that job.

US SKU: QSW-2104-2S-US / QSW-2104-2S-A-US. Two 10G SFP+ plus four 2.5G RJ45. Maker max 12 W. Fanless ventilated metal. Class B. Distinct from the CRS305 (SFP+ only) and from the 8-port 2.5G unmanaged myth post. The 8-port copper shopping list is still 2.5G switch.

Amazon ASIN B0BFC6QFRL snap $116. B&H listing $79.99 (was $129.99, $50 off) when fetched. Older Amazon ASIN B09B9CJQ2D exists for the same model family. Confirm the US letter suffix.

Skip

Skip the used Cisco 10G Catalyst / Nexus for this mix of ports. Skip expecting lifetime paper. QNAP is two years on the hardware spec. Skip a random used optic; buy a new DAC in DAC + named X710. The used-vs-new heater test is still here.

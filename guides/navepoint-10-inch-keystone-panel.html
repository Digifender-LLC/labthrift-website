A blank face for jacks you buy separately: NavePoint keystone panel

A blank face for jacks you buy separately. The TRENDnet punch-down stays the Cat6 default. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. Skip this unless you need HDMI, USB, fiber, or coax in the same 1U.

SKU 00407268 is a 10-inch 12-port keystone plate for the NavePoint 6U. 0.461 × 10 × 1.764 in. ANSI/TIA/EIA 568B. Maker store $27.59 this pass. Walmart (sold by NavePoint) printed the same dollar. Amazon B0CH1H22VY is live. No jacks in the box. TC-P12C6 is twelve Cat6 punches, 110 or Krone, maker $28.59.

The modular panel looks like the flexible buy next to the cabinet. It is a faceplate. You still buy twelve keystones, or a mix of HDMI / USB / coax / fiber, and you still terminate each one. For a closet of Cat6 drops that is more SKUs and more dollars than one punch-down. Depth 0.461 in will hang in the NavePoint 7 in max mount with room left. Width is the real 10-inch / 254 mm, not a 19-inch blank keystone sold as “compact.”

Newegg item 2BA-001H-002H7 is the same card; no clean street dollar on this fetch. Print the live tag. The STP punch-down sibling is 00407266, also a skip for most closets.

Skip

Skip 00407268 because the face is empty and the dollar looks close to TC-P12C6. Skip it as a “starter” panel you will fill later with random keystones. Skip 19-inch blank keystone faces sold next to this one. You still need a punch-down or a keystone tool depending on what you load; the empty face does not replace the Klein VDV427-300 for Cat6 IDC. Used eBay lots are out. I haven't loaded a 00407268.

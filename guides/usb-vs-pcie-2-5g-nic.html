Meet a faster closet switch without swapping the mini PC

Most tiny living-room boxes ship a normal house ethernet port. A $20–$30 adapter is the cheapest way to meet a faster closet switch without buying a new computer.

Meet a faster closet switch (2.5G, faster than normal house ethernet) without swapping the mini PC. PCIe is the cleaner Proxmox path if a slot exists. USB (Realtek RTL8156B / RTL8156BG) is the path when it does not. See the N100 note before you assume the box already has 2.5G.

Skip

Skip a host swap just to get 2.5G. Skip unnamed USB dongles with no chipset on the page. Don't jump to 10G until a single flow fills 2.5. That is the 10Gtek vs TX201 note.

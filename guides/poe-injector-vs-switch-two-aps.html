One injector, two injectors, or a 4-port PoE+ switch

One AP can use a small injector. Two APs is when a tiny PoE switch starts making sense. Don't buy four injectors.

Cheap PoE for two APs already crowned TL-SG1005P. This post is the injector math. A 15.4 W af injector feeds EAP613 (10.9 W), U7 Lite (~13 W), Instant On AP21/AP22, or GWN7660 (9 W). It does not feed EAP653 (15.5 W, 802.3at). One at-class puck needs a 30 W injector or a switch port that actually does at.

Two af APs fit one 64 W fanless 4-PoE+ switch. Two injectors plus a non-PoE switch is more wall warts and the same money as the switch once you buy the second brick. UniFi’s own 15 W injector is $8 if you already live there.

If the APs are 2.5G (U7 Lite, Cudy AP3000, EAP670), the injector still works: the uplink is only as fast as the injector’s gigabit jack. For 2.5G end to end you want 8-port 2.5G PoE. Match class in af / at / bt. Don't feed any of these from a 24 V passive brick.

Skip

Skip a second 150S when the next AP is at-class. Skip SG2210MP for two APs, 150 W and a fan you will hear; fan note. Skip treating two $18 injectors as cheaper than a $52 fanless switch if you already know camera three is coming.

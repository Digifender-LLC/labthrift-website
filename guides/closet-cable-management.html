Written 22 Aug 2026. Specs from the maker pages linked below. Prices from those stores. I haven't used these.

Hook-and-loop, not zip ties, in a closet

Zip ties are how you cut a DAC jacket six months later. A 100-pack of 8-inch hook-and-loop is the whole cable-management budget.

Color-code power vs copper vs DAC. Revisit after every add. Put a TZe flag on the lead before you bundle it. This lives on the 12U 2-post, not behind the drywall.

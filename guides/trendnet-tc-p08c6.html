Four fewer punches to save a few dollars: TRENDnet TC-P08C6

Four fewer punches to save a few dollars. The 12-port stays the 10-inch default. 10-inch is a skinny wall cabinet. Skip the 8-port unless you counted eight drops and will not add a ninth.

SKU TC-P08C6 is the 8-port sibling of the TC-P12C6. Same 254 mm / 10 in width. Same 1.7 × 1.2 in face. Maker store $21.99 this pass. Amazon B003SSX1Z2 printed $14.99. The 12-port is still maker $28.59.

V2 card: 8 × 1000Base-T RJ-45, 250 MHz Cat6, T568A/T568B, 110 or Krone IDC, labels, holders and ties in the box. 254 × 44 × 30 mm. 205 g. NDAA. 3-year limited. Depth 1.2 in, so it hangs in the NavePoint 7 in max mount. It is still not a 19-inch panel. The Cable Matters 180010 still will not fit this cabinet, 8 ports or 12.

Amazon $14.99 looks like a deal next to the 12-port at $28.59. That is the trap. You spend the punch-down once. Extra keystone later is another 1U and another tool session. If you already hung the 6U, buy twelve punches. Print the live Amazon tag. That dollar moves.

Skip

Skip the 8-port because Amazon is $14.99. Skip it as a “starter” panel you will outgrow in one punch session. Skip any 19-inch 8-port or 12-port sold next to this one. You still need a 110 or Krone tool; it is not in the TRENDnet box. Use the Klein VDV427-300. Used eBay lots are out. I haven't punched a TC-P08C6.

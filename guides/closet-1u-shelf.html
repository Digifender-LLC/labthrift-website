A 1U shelf so the NAS is not sitting on the UPS

Mini PCs and 2-bay NAS boxes are not 19-inch. Hang a 10-inch vented 1U cantilever on the 12U frame. The 7-inch sibling is too short for a DS224+.

Park the pizza boxes on steel, not on the UPS lid and not on the carpet. Hang this on the StarTech 12U 2-post. Plug the metal 1U strip into the UPS, not the other way around.

Skip

Skip the 7-inch CABSHELF1U / CABSHELF1U7V (Markertek $39.65 on the vented 7-inch). A DS224+ is 232.2 mm deep. Skip particle-board “rack” trays. Skip parking the NAS on the UPS.

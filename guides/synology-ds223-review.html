Quiet overnight backups on DSM without the Plus tax: DS223

Quiet overnight backups and photo sync on Synology DSM without paying the Plus tax. Skip Plus if that is the whole job. Skip the j either way.

Quiet overnight backups and photo sync on Synology DSM without paying the Plus tax. This is the Value 2-bay when sleep backups alone are the job: Hyper Backup, Photos, a quiet shelf. Not Container Manager, not a VM host. Specs and the Plus fork follow.

Realtek RTD1619B, four cores at 1.7 GHz, hardware encryption on. 2 GB DDR4 soldered, no slot. Two 3.5" / 2.5" SATA bays. One 1GbE, MTU 1500. Three USB 3.2 Gen 1 plus a front copy button. 92 mm fan. 165 × 108 × 232.7 mm, 1.28 kg. 60 W brick. Maker numbers: 17.343 W access, 4.08 W HDD hibernation, 14.6 dB(A) idle with Synology SSDs at 1 m. Two-year hardware warranty. Btrfs, SHR or RAID 1. Two free Surveillance Station licenses. That is a backup appliance, not a Docker host.

Technobezz printed Amazon $252.43 against a $284.99 strike on 9 Jun 2026. BigGo still showed OEM listings around $286.99 in Aug 2026. Treat mid-$250s as sale weather and ~$285 as the boring street. Buy the diskless chassis. Drives are a second bill: CMR NAS disks, not a reseller kit. The first-NAS math is in the roundup. The Plus sibling is DS224+.

Skip

Skip DS223j when the real DS223 is on the page. Skip any Amazon title that packs two drives: you want B0BRNBVTJK, diskless. Skip this chassis the day you want Container Manager, VMM, or 2.5G; that is Plus or a UGREEN, not Value. Skip leftover desktop HDDs in SHR. Skip using the single 1GbE as a VM datastore. Two bays is not a media library that grows for a decade. If you already know you want four, DS423+ or the DXP2800 2-bay with 2.5G.

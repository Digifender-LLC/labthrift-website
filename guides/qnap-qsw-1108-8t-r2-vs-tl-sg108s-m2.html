QSW-1108-8T-R2 vs TL-SG108S-M2: same eight ports, usually more money

Eight quiet faster ports. Skip the QNAP unless you already live in QNAP and the price is close.

Same eight faster ports as the cheaper TP-Link. Skip the QNAP unless the price is close. QSW-1108-8T-R2: eight 2.5GbE RJ-45 (2.5G / 1G / 100M), unmanaged, fanless, 12 V / 1 A wall brick. Maker max 12 W. 4K MAC, 40 Gbps fabric, 20 Gbps non-blocking, 12K jumbo. Loop detect with a dedicated LED. 26 × 160 × 110 mm. QNAP says this R2 is 30% smaller than the last chassis. Two-year paper. This is not QSW-2104-2S (that one has two 10G SFP+ cages). It is also not the older non-R2 QSW-1108-8T listings still floating at specialty shops.

QNAP US store, 23 Aug 2026: $119.00, labeled in stock, with a 2–4 week lead-time note on the same card. Print the live tag. Don't invent an Amazon ASIN. NeutronUSA still lists the pre-R2 SKU around $245. That is not this buy, and it is not a reason to wait.

Skip

Skip QSW-1108-8T-R2 because the NAS is QNAP. A switch does not have to match the NAS logo. Skip waiting two to four weeks for eight 2.5G ports. Skip the older non-R2 QSW-1108-8T when a shop still has it at $200+. Skip eBay lots. If you actually needed two 10G SFP+ cages, buy QSW-2104-2S. If you counted under six endpoints, the 5-port default is TL-SG105S-M2.

Skip

Skip the HP as a primary closet buy. Valid SKU, wrong room. Skip 24-port multi-gig PoE racks (heat, fans, 300–700 W bricks). Skip SODOLA PoE toys. If you only have two APs and no 10G cage, the unmanaged TL-SG105PP-M2 is the smaller hop. 1G path: cheap PoE.
